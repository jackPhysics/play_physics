Basic instructions for using the Stimemorulators

Pressing the "Go" button will flash up a formula inside the text-box.

You must then re-enter that formula EXACTLY as it appeared.

You can re-enter the formula by...

  • Typing directly into the text-box;


  • Using the buttons of the keypad.


When you think you have the right formula press "TEST" and see if you are "RIGHT!" or "WRONG!"

Score +1 for every correct answer, -1 for every wrong answer.

Keep going! Try to work out what the formula must be using your knowledge and memory.

The formulae are repeated; have you seen it before? Do you REMEMBER it?
