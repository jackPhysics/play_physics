  • 'Common Sense' Laws - means the cursor simply moves Dragon, it changes Dragon's 'displacement'
  • 'Gas pedal' Laws - the cursor speeds Dragon up the longer the cursor is held down, when the cursor is let go Dragon keeps some speed but then glides to a halt.
  • Newton's Laws of Motion - means the cursor adds a velocity vector, it changes Dragon's 'velocity'.
