Notes for producing Transverse waves

  1. Watch the beads - which way are they moving?
    But which way is the 'movement' moving?
  2. Use the "show lines" button to help you study the movement of the beads.
  3. You are only shaking the left hand end of the string - but does the right hand end of the string shake? WHY?
  4. Want to make a perfect wave (a sine wave)? Sure you do! Here's how:... Grab the end of the string and move the mouse in a CIRCLE. What do you get?
  5. What sort of wave do you get if you just move the mouse up and down?
  6. What sort of wave do you get if you just move the mouse from side to side?
