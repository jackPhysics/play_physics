Notes for producing Longitudinal waves

  1. Watch the beads - which way are they moving?
    And which way is the 'movement' moving?
  2. Are the beads moving in the same direction as the 'movement' or in the opposite direction?
  3. Use the "show lines" button to help you study the movement of the beads.
    Can you return the beads back to their starting position?
  4. You are only pushing/pulling the left hand side - but does the right hand side get pushed? WHY?
  5. Want to make a perfect wave? Sure you do! Here's how:... Grab the first layer and move the mouse in a CIRCLE. What do you get?
  6. Does it look 'perfect'? How can you tell?!
  7. How fast do waves travel through this air?
  8. Can you measure their frequency and wavelength? Can you even see their frequency and wavelength?!

EXERCISES

  1. Make a wave with an amplitude of 2 cm...
  2. Make a wave with a wavelength of 5 cm...
  3. Make a wave with a frequency of 1 hz...
  4. What is the velocity of waves through these layers of air?
